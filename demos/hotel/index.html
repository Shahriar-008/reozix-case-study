About Reozix Hotel

Reozix Hotel is a 28-room boutique stay set on the Mornington Peninsula, where the vineyard belt meets the sea. Each room is individually designed — expect king beds, freestanding baths, and views over the vines or the water rather than a chain-standard template. We operate entirely on direct booking: best rate guaranteed, late checkout until 1pm, and a welcome drink from the Mornington cellar on arrival. When you book direct, the room is exactly what you see and the relationship stays with the property.

Location

We sit between the Mornington Peninsula's two best attractions: a ten-minute walk to the beach and a five-minute drive to the cellar doors of the main ridge. The Peninsula is an hour and a half from Melbourne — wine tastings in the afternoon, a dip in the bay before dinner. We're happy to arrange tastings, coastal walks, and local transfers at the front desk.
